Learning Business & Finance

From the clinic to the boardroom — and all the interesting spaces in between.

BDS graduate with a growing interest in the intersection of healthcare, business, finance and strategy.

My background in dentistry has given me exposure to healthcare, patient interaction, problem-solving and the realities of working within a healthcare ecosystem. I am now expanding my knowledge beyond clinical dentistry and building a stronger foundation in business and management.

Currently, I am developing skills in business fundamentals, finance, data analysis, Excel, communication and strategic thinking while preparing for the next stage of my academic and professional journey.

I am particularly interested in exploring how business, technology and strategy can create better outcomes in healthcare and other industries.

I believe in continuous learning and intentionally building my career one skill at a time.
